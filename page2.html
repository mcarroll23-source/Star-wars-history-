Star Wars Sith

What Are the Sith?

The Sith are a group of Force-sensitive individuals in the Star Wars universe who use the dark side of the Force.

The Sith seek power and often believe that strength and control are necessary to achieve their goals.

The Sith and the Dark Side

The Sith embrace emotions such as anger, fear, and hatred as sources of power. Their beliefs are very different from the Jedi philosophy.

Throughout Star Wars history, different Sith have attempted to gain control over others and the galaxy.

Important Sith

  1. Darth Sidious
  2. Darth Vader
  3. Darth Maul
  4. Count Dooku
  5. Darth Revan
  6. Darth Bane
