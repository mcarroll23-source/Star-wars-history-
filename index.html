Star Wars Jedi

What Are the Jedi?

The Jedi are an organization of Force-sensitive individuals in the Star Wars universe who work to protect peace and justice throughout the galaxy.

Jedi are trained to use the Force and lightsabers while following principles that encourage discipline, knowledge, and service to others.

Important Jedi

  • Yoda
  • Obi-Wan Kenobi
  • Luke Skywalker
  • Mace Windu
  • Anakin Skywalker
  • Qui-Gon Jinn
  • Kit Fisto
